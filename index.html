Feature 1: Fast barcode checkout

Live In OctaBiz before the 24 Oct 2026 proposal; UPC-A labels from 1 Nov, Enter adds the item from 14 Nov 2026

1The request

Ring up a grocery basket by scanning: every product barcode, the US 12-digit UPC-A price and weight labels that scales print, and a code the cashier types when a label won't scan.

2The problem it solves

At a busy register, every item that fails to scan sends the cashier to search by name and pick from a list while the line waits. Price and weight labels from the produce or deli scale use a different barcode layout from packaged goods, so the till has to read both.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. POS
  2. Cashier Terminal

In POS › Cashier Terminal the cashier scans into the search and scan bar. A product barcode puts the item in the cart, and a UPC-A price or weight label rings up the weighed amount in the product's own unit. When a label is torn, the cashier types the SKU or barcode and presses Enter: the item goes in the cart and the box clears for the next one. Typing a PLU and pressing Enter opens Weigh & Sell.

Feature 2: Till count and shift report

Live In OctaBiz before the 24 Oct 2026 proposal; EBT lines from 2 Nov, every sale in Session details from 14 Nov 2026

1The request

Each cashier opens and closes their own till, counts the drawer at the end of the shift, and gets an X report during the day and a Z report at close, with every tender on its own line.

2The problem it solves

Six stores with several tills each: when nobody compares the drawer count with what the till expected, a shortage turns up days later in the bank deposit and nobody can say which shift it came from. EBT and card money never sits in the drawer, so it must not be counted as cash.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. POS
  2. Sessions
  1. POS
  2. Cashier Terminal
  3. Close till

The till asks the cashier to count the drawer before signing out (Close till). The close shows Expected cash, Counted cash and Over / short, and the X and Z reports give each tender its own line. EBT Food and EBT Cash show as owed by the EBT processor and stay out of the drawer count. Any over or short is booked under that store. POS › Sessions lists every shift by store and till, and Session details shows every sale in it and can reprint or resend a receipt.

Feature 3: Special orders and holding items

Live 2 Nov 2026

1The request

Take a special order for a product the store doesn't stock, tied to the customer's record, with a deposit, the right tax and a message when it arrives; and park a sale at one till so another till in the same store can finish it.

2The problem it solves

A customer asks for a case of a supplement the store doesn't carry. On paper the phone number gets lost, nobody remembers to call when the box arrives, and the deposit is hard to find at pickup. A sale parked at one till could only be finished on that same till.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. POS
  2. Layaways
  3. New
  4. Special order

POS › Layaways (also found by searching Special orders) has New with Type set to Special order. The cashier picks or creates the customer, adds catalog products, each taxed by its own tax code, and takes a deposit. When the goods come in, Mark arrived and tell the customer sends a ready-to-collect message by email, or WhatsApp when it is connected. A parked sale stays on the server for 7 days with the name of whoever parked it, and any till in that store can open it; two tills can't ring the same sale twice.

Feature 4: Tax per item

Live 2 Nov 2026 (tax on each line from 1 Nov)

1The request

Each item carries its own tax code and rate at the till, so groceries, supplements and other goods are taxed differently, and the sales tax report files sales and refunds by rate and by tax agency.

2The problem it solves

A grocery with several tax rates can't file from a report that puts every till sale under one default rate. Each filing period someone rebuilds the split from receipts in a spreadsheet, and a product added without a tax code is easy to miss.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. Reports
  2. Sales tax
  1. POS
  2. Books check

Every till line keeps its product's tax code and rate, and refunds follow the same split. Reports › Sales tax has a row for each rate and agency, a row for exempt or zero-rated sales, and SNAP purchases on their own exempt row. A product with no tax code sells under the business default tax, and POS › Books check lists those products under Products with no tax code, each with a Set tax code button.

Feature 5: Gift cards

Live 2 Nov 2026

1The request

Sell new gift cards and top up existing ones at the till, keep them as a liability in the books, and print the remaining balance on the receipt.

2The problem it solves

Gift cards sold at the register but tracked somewhere else drift away from the books: a sold card isn't revenue until it is spent, and a voided sale has to take the card's value back. Customers also ask at every visit how much is left on their card.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. POS
  2. Cashier Terminal
  3. Gift card
  1. Accounting
  2. Sales
  3. Gift cards

In POS › Cashier Terminal, the Gift card button in the cart sells a new card or adds money to a customer's card. The cashier scans or types the card number, and a blank new card gets one. The card line is never taxed or discounted. The sale debits cash or card and credits Gift card liability, the receipt prints the card's balance, and voiding the sale takes the value back. Money on a gift card is never refunded as cash. Cards need the internet, so the button is off while the till is offline. Every card is listed under Accounting › Sales › Gift cards. In a live test a card was sold, topped up and voided, and every account went back to zero.

Feature 6: Scales and weighed items

Live 2 Nov 2026

1The request

Sell produce, bulk and deli items by weight: read the weight from the scale built into the checkout scanner, read the price and weight labels that label scales print, and send each product's real price to those scales.

2The problem it solves

Bulk bins and produce are a large share of a natural grocery's sales. Typing weights by hand is slow and every mistake costs money, and a label scale still holding last month's price prints labels that disagree with the till.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. POS
  2. Cashier Terminal
  3. Weigh & Sell
  1. POS
  2. PLU Catalog

The till reads a steady weight from the checkout scanner-scale (USB or RS-232, for example Datalogic Magellan models) and converts it to the product's own unit; a weight that is still moving never reaches the cart. Price and weight labels in the US UPC-A layout ring up at the till, pound labels included. POS › PLU Catalog makes the PLU file for Hobart, Mettler Toledo, DIGI and Bizerba label scales with each product's real price, PLU, tare and shelf life. The receipt prints the weight with its unit and the price per kilo or pound.

Feature 7: EBT/SNAP payments

Live 2 Nov 2026

1The request

Take EBT Food (SNAP) and EBT Cash at the till: flag SNAP-eligible products, charge no sales tax on the share SNAP pays, send refunds back to the same tender, and keep SNAP sales on their own row in the tax report.

2The problem it solves

In a US grocery, SNAP pays only for eligible items, and the part it pays carries no sales tax. Working that out by hand on a mixed basket is slow and error-prone, and a SNAP refund handed back as cash breaks the program's rules.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. POS
  2. Cashier Terminal
  3. Pay
  4. EBT
  1. POS
  2. Settings

Products have a SNAP eligible switch in the product editor and in bulk edit. At Pay › EBT the till shows SNAP can pay up to, counting only eligible items after discounts. The cashier runs the card on the store's standalone EBT terminal, types the approved amount and the approval number, and the tax on the SNAP share drops off. Refunds split by tender, so a SNAP purchase never comes back as cash. The X and Z reports give EBT Food and EBT Cash their own lines, and the sales tax report has a SNAP purchases (exempt) row. It works offline too. In a live test sale, EBT Food paid the whole basket, the tax came to zero, and the books recorded the amount as owed by the EBT processor.

Feature 9: Selling when the internet is down

Live 2 Nov 2026 (tested offline on the live site); more safeguards from 16 Nov 2026

1The request

Keep selling when the store's internet drops, and send every sale to the books when the connection returns, with nothing lost.

2The problem it solves

A dropped connection on a busy morning can't stop the registers. Cashiers need to keep ringing up cash sales, open a till without internet, and trust that every sale is stored on the device until it reaches the books.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. POS
  2. Cashier Terminal
  1. POS
  2. Download apps

The Cashier Terminal keeps selling offline. Cash and EBT sales are saved on the device before the sale shows as paid, and they post to the books when the connection returns. A till can be opened offline from the header. If the device can't store sales, a red strip reading Sales aren't being saved on this till tells the cashier to keep the page open. Gift cards and Rewards points need the internet and say so while offline. For a register that must start with no connection at all, POS › Download apps has the desktop till app for Mac and Windows. On the live site, a cash sale rung up offline posted to the books once the connection came back.

2The problem it solves

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. POS

Feature 12: Discounts and promotions

Live 4 Nov 2026 (codes and deals 3 Nov; department sales and member prices 4 Nov)

1The request

Run grocery promotions at the till: "3 for $5", mix and match across products or a category, buy X get Y, a limit per sale, dated department sales and member prices, plus discount codes that respect their dates and limits.

2The problem it solves

Without deals at the till, cashiers key discounts by hand and each one is a chance to give away too much, while a cashier without discount rights can't sell the store's own promotions. A code that has ended or been used up must not get through, even when two people use it at the same moment.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. Sales
  2. Promotions
  1. POS
  2. Cashier Terminal

Sales › Promotions has the offer types Buy N for a price, Any N of these for a price (by product or category), Buy X get Y and Department sale, each with dates, stores and an optional limit per sale. A What the customer pays table shows the price for 1 to 7 units. At the till the deal comes off by itself, the line names it, Why this price? explains it and the receipt prints the saving. A member pays the price from their price list, and the till always charges the lowest price per item without stacking. A department sale never charges more than the shelf label. Codes that have ended, haven't started, are used up or need a bigger order show greyed out with the reason, such as Ended Nov 2.

Feature 17: EDI ordering with UNFI and KeHE

Live 12 Nov 2026 (mailbox checked every 15 minutes from 14 Nov)

1The request

Send purchase orders to UNFI and KeHE by EDI and take their answers back in: order confirmations (855), ship notices (856), invoices (810) and acknowledgements (997).

2The problem it solves

Large distributors expect EDI. Without it, orders go by email or portal, confirmations and short shipments are read by eye, and every invoice is typed in and checked against the delivery by hand.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. Resources
  2. Vendors
  3. a vendor
  4. EDI messages
  1. Procurement
  2. Purchase Orders
  3. Send PO

A vendor set up for EDI (the vendor's Settings › Electronic ordering (EDI)) gets Send by EDI in the Send PO dialog, which drops an X12 850 order into the distributor's SFTP mailbox. OctaBiz checks that mailbox every 15 minutes, and the vendor's EDI messages tab also has Check for messages and Add a file you received. An 855 confirms the order with their delivery date and holds any cut lines for the buyer to accept. An 856 becomes a delivery batch on its way, with lots and expiry dates. An 810 becomes a draft bill that has been through the 3-way match of ordered, received and billed. Everything can run in Testing mode first.

Store step: EDI IDs and trading partner agreements with UNFI and KeHE.

Feature 18: Distributor catalogs and price files

Live 9 Nov 2026

1The request

Load UNFI and KeHE price files on a schedule straight from their SFTP servers, warn when a new cost squeezes the margin, and use the distributor's promo cost on orders while it runs.

2The problem it solves

Distributors change costs every few weeks. When someone downloads and imports the price file by hand, costs go stale, and nobody notices that a product now sells under its target margin until the month's numbers come in.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. Resources
  2. Vendors
  3. a vendor
  4. Products
  5. Scheduled and Margins

On a vendor's Products tab, the Scheduled sub-tab takes SFTP server as a source: server, port, user name and a folder or file name pattern such as /outgoing/prices_*.csv, with a password or a private key. The newest matching CSV, Excel or zipped file is imported on schedule. The Margins sub-tab then lists products whose new cost leaves them under the owner's margin (30% unless changed) or below zero, at each store's selling price. Promo cost, start and end columns are read from the file, and Quick PO uses the promo cost while it runs (Promo until 30 Nov). The import changes only the supplier's cost; stock values and selling prices stay as they are.

Feature 19: AI order forecasting

Live 9 Nov 2026

1The request

Suggest each store's order from that store's own sales, with the effect of seasons, holidays and planned promotions, on a review screen where the buyer accepts or changes each line before it becomes a purchase order.

2The problem it solves

Ordering from last week's sales misses the busy weeks: a holiday or a promotion empties the shelf, and the order placed afterwards arrives too late. A buyer can't do that arithmetic for thousands of items across six stores.

3In OctaBiz: where and how

  1. Inventory
  2. Reorder
  3. Forecast review

Inventory › Reorder › Forecast review works on one store at a time. For every item sold there it shows on hand, on order, what it should sell until the next order arrives, a suggested order capped at the store's max stock and rounded to full cases, and a short reason such as Thanksgiving week +30%. The buyer accepts, changes or skips each line, and Add to draft PO fills one draft per supplier for that store. How we got this shows every step of the arithmetic. Explain this suggestion asks AI to put the same numbers into a few sentences; the numbers themselves never depend on AI. Days when the item was out of stock are left out, so a stock-out doesn't drag the forecast down.

Feature 25: Shrink tracking

1The request

2The problem it solves

Damaged, expired and stolen goods are a real cost in a grocery, but if they only show up as count differences, the owner can't tell breakage from theft or spoilage, and can't act on any of them.
